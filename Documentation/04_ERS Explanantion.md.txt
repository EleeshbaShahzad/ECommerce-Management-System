ERD Explanation
Overview
The ERD (Entity Relationship Diagram) represents the structure of the E-Commerce Management & Analytics System database.
It visually explains:
 Database tables
 Primary Keys
 Foreign Keys
 Relationships
 Business workflow

Main Entities
Customers
Stores customer information.
Categories
Stores product categories.
Products
Stores product inventory and product catalog.
Orders
Stores customer order records.
OrderDetails
Stores products included in each order.
Payments
Stores order payment information.
Shipping
Stores delivery and shipping tracking details.
Reviews
Stores customer product reviews and ratings.

Relationship Explanation

Customers → Orders
One customer can place multiple orders.
Relationship:
1 → N

Orders → OrderDetails
One order can contain multiple products.
Relationship:
1 → N

Products → OrderDetails
One product can appear in multiple orders.
Relationship:
1 → N

Categories → Products
One category can contain multiple products.
Relationship:
1 → N

Orders → Payments
Each order has one payment transaction.
Relationship:
1 → 1

Orders → Shipping
Each order has one shipping record.
Relationship:
1 → 1

 Customers → Reviews
One customer can give multiple reviews.
Relationship:
1 → N

Products → Reviews
One product can receive multiple reviews.
Relationship:
1 → N

 Database Design Goals
The database is designed to:
 Maintain data integrity
 Reduce data redundancy
 Improve query performance
 Support analytical reporting
 Simulate real-world e-commerce workflows

Business Workflow
1. Customer registers
2. Products added to catalog
3. Customer places order
4. Order details recorded
5. Payment processed
6. Inventory updated
7. Shipping initiated
8. Customer leaves review
9. Analytics generated

 Analytics Support
The database supports:
 Revenue analysis
 Customer segmentation
 Product performance tracking
 Inventory analysis
 Sales trend reporting
 Payment analysis
