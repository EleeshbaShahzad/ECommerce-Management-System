 Requirement Analysis


Business Overview
The E-Commerce Management & Analytics System is designed to simulate a real-world online shopping platform.
The system manages:
- Customer accounts
- Product catalog
- Product categories
- Inventory tracking
- Customer orders
- Order details
- Payment processing
- Shipping management
- Product reviews
- Business analytics

Business Goals
The system should:
- Track customer purchases
- Manage inventory automatically
- Process online orders
- Handle payments securely
- Analyze sales performance
- Monitor top-selling products
- Identify valuable customers
- Generate business KPIs

 Main Business Workflow
1. Customer registers
2. Customer browses products
3. Customer places order
4. Order details recorded
5. Payment processed
6. Inventory updated
7. Shipping initiated
8. Customer leaves review
9. Sales analytics generated

 User Roles

Admin
Controls complete platform.

Customer
Places orders and reviews products.

Warehouse Staff
Handles inventory and shipping.

Finance Team
Tracks payments and revenue.

Data Analyst
Generates business insights and reports.

Analytics Requirements
The system should support:
 Monthly revenue analysis
 Top-selling products
 Customer segmentation
 Product performance analysis
 Payment method analysis
 Inventory trend analysis
 Repeat customer tracking

 Technical Requirements
The database should include:
 Primary Keys
 Foreign Keys
 Constraints
 Stored Procedures
 Triggers
 Transactions
 Indexing
 Views
 Analytical SQL Queries

 Project Objective
To build a production-style SQL database system that combines:
 Database Development
 Backend SQL Logic
 Business Analytics
 Performance Optimization
