Entities and Relationships

 Main Entities

 Customers
Stores customer information.

Categories
Stores product categories.

Products
Stores product details and inventory.

Orders
Stores customer orders.

OrderDetails
Stores products included in each order.

Payments
Stores payment transactions.

Shipping
Stores shipping and delivery details.

Reviews
Stores customer product reviews.

Relationships

One Customer → Many Orders
A customer can place multiple orders.

One Order → Many OrderDetails
An order can contain multiple products.

One Product → Many OrderDetails
A product can appear in multiple orders.

One Category → Many Products
A category can contain multiple products.

One Order → One Payment
Each order has one payment transaction.

One Order → One Shipping Record
Each order has shipping details.

One Customer → Many Reviews
Customers can review multiple products.

One Product → Many Reviews
Products can receive multiple reviews.
